THE FULL PROCESS

From a photo on your phone to a decision in the field

Four steps, no internet required for the scan itself. Here's exactly what happens to a photo you upload, and what the assistant does with a question you type or speak.

1

Take or upload a photo

Pick a single affected leaf, fill the frame, and shoot in daylight against a plain background. Tap the upload area below, or use your phone's camera app — JPG or PNG, under 10MB.

2

On-device analysis

The photo never has to leave your phone. A lightweight color-and-texture model scans the leaf for lesions, discoloration, and healthy tissue in under two seconds.

3

Match against the crop database

The result is checked against known pest and disease signatures for the crop you selected, and ranked by how closely it matches.

4

Ask the assistant a follow-up

Not sure what the diagnosis means, or when to irrigate? Type or speak your question to the assistant — it answers using the same crop data, in your language.
